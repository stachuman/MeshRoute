## Organizer checklist

1. Inventory the devices and assign each one to a person or fixed location.
2. Choose the appropriate firmware role for every device.
3. Establish the required network and team configuration.
4. Handle identifiers and keys safely.
5. Verify every device individually.
6. Run an end-to-end message and inbox test involving every participant.
7. Record the recovery information participants may safely carry.
