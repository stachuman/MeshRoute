## Scope — the catalog reaches the panel
- **The compose lists render ENABLED slots** in stable-slot order (gaps valid; scroll when enabled > visible),
  each row carrying its **stable slot id** — ⛔ never derived from the row index (§B66; the fixed
  `kDmTexts`/`kChannelTexts` tables RETIRE this slice — replaced by the catalog projection, with their
  retirement recorded in place). DM slots never in the channel list and vice versa. `back, don't send` stays
  the DERIVED final row (an action, not a slot).
- **The row format**: selection marker · **`L` or `-`** (always one of the two — the OQ-A ruling's premise) ·
  the text (≤17 by validation). The wearer confirms the location marker as part of the double press.
- **`SendReq{slot, generation}` replaces index identity** (the design's §3.3 freeze): the press seals the slot
  AND the generation displayed; at execution, a disabled slot or a moved generation ⇒ **`PRESET CHANGED`**
  (the ruled visible word) with ZERO core submission, then repaint from the current catalog. Page-buffer
  painting freezes one generation per frame (no torn list).
- **The per-kind send compositions** (§3.2.3's table + R-2's truth table): emergency — catalog text, `-l` iff
  loc=on AND a fix exists, sends regardless (R-3/§4.1); channel — `send_channel <ch> "<text>" -t [-l] -e`,
  loc=on with no fix/key/seal ⇒ the LOUD refusal, ⛔ `-l` never stripped; DM — `send <id> "<text>" -t -a [-l]`,
  loc=on requires the sealed path, ⛔ no downgrade. The EMERGENCY long-press reads the catalog's emergency
  text (retiring `kEmergencyText`'s direct use — same recorded retirement).
- **The modal-close half of the §2 table**: a successful CHANGED mutation (incl. `reset all`) closes an open
  selection-phase compose without sending; a no-op or a failure does NOT close it; an already-displayed
  outcome may finish. The trigger is the generation move (P2's threading decision — no new hook).
- **The zero-enabled empty state** (§3.2.1's) with the back row only.
- **R-1**: K7's `GRANT KEY` row byte-identical — position (between the enabled DM slots and the back row),
  gating, semantics; its landed cases and controls re-run untouched.
