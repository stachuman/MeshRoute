## 3. The cross-mobile refusal (spec §4.3, F3) — exact edits and their consequence

- Remove the `e.mobile_hash == _deleg_acks[slot].mobile_hash` term at `node_hashlocate.cpp:1951`
  (`deleg_ack_activation_available`) and the `e.mobile_hash == mobile_hash` term of the ACTIVE-match arm at
  `:1993` (`deleg_ack_put`). The in-source C1 notes above both lines (`:1946-1952`, `:1990-1991`) must be replaced,
  not left as false history.
- **Two tests flip, not one**: `test/test_dual_layer.cpp:3975` ("two hosted-mobile hashes sharing ctrH and return
  peer cannot cross-correlate" — currently asserts both `deleg_ack_put` succeed) and the second half of `:4263`
  ("activation uniqueness stays SAME-MOBILE-SCOPED", which asserts `live_n == 2` for two mobiles).
- **Mutation D09 becomes the production code** (`tools/probe_ui_model_mutations.py`, MUTS_B251HASH). Retire it and
  add its inverse: re-adding the `mobile_hash` term must be RED. D12 ("a RESERVED row is born with a custody
  candidate") also becomes production for eligible flights and must be re-scoped (e.g. candidate set for a
  non-eligible carrier, or `eligible` written on a cleared arm).
- **Consequence on the wrapper path, which the brief must state**: a refusal at activation there is a silent
  mapping loss. `commit_deleg_ack` returns `false`, `release_deleg_ack()` frees the reservation, the caller discards
  the result (`(void)commit_deleg_ack(...)` at `:1703`/`:1792`/`:1821`/`:1836`), and the outward DM still flies.
  The returning ACK then reaches the last-mile fork with no row, is forwarded untranslated (the home's `ctr_h`,
  `node_mac_rx.cpp:1838-1848`), and the mobile times out with `e2e_ack_timeout`. For direct transit the refusal is
  pre-ACK (`deleg_ack_activation_available` at `:1418` → BUSY_RX reason 2), i.e. loud. Home counters are
  per-destination (`node_mac.cpp:20-32`), so no natural collision exists; the guard is against a wrap or a
  malformed peer. The brief should require a test of the wrapper-path outcome as it IS, and may propose (owner
  ruling, not S1b scope) whether the wrapper arm should signal the mobile via `presence_mark_deleg_fail` as the
  XL arm does (`node_mac_rx.cpp:1761`).
