# Team Encrypted Channel — design spec (2026-07-26, ★ RATIFIED — all §4 questions answered by the owner 2026-07-26; dispatch-ready)

## 0. Summary & motivation

Team MEMBERSHIP is open-by-knowledge (anyone with the team parameters can team-DAD in — by design).
Therefore membership must NOT imply the ability to READ the team's traffic. This spec separates the two
privileges: a **team channel keypair** (X25519 pub+priv), generated by the team creator, encrypts team
channel posts (text AND an optional location payload); the keypair is distributed **deliberately, never
automatically** — via (a) a new sealed DM type from any keyholder to a vetted newjoiner, or (b) an
out-of-band **team QR** from the iOS companion carrying the full team provisioning (PHY parameters) plus
the keypair in one scan. An overlay-member without the key still relays the team's floods (content-blind)
but cannot read them.

Trust model (deliberate, simple): **holding team_priv = full read+write+grant rights.** Any keyholder can
grant onward (the owner's ruling: "anyone can send priv key"). There is no in-protocol revocation — a
compromised/departed member is handled by RE-KEYING (generate a new pair, re-grant to the remaining
members; old posts stay readable to the old key — forward secrecy is explicitly out of scope for v1).

## 1. Present state (code-verified 2026-07-26)

- **The M frame reserved this from day one**: byte 2 `flavor` — "0=public plaintext, 1=group/encrypted, …
  — encryption deferred" (frame_codec.h:574). Team scoping is already a flavor bit (`channel_flavor_team`,
  +4 B team_id, M_FRAME_TEAM_HDR_LEN=11, frame_codec.h:578).
- **Channels are cleartext today**: `Inbox::record_channel` hardcodes enc=0 (inbox.h:100) — but the durable
  record FORMAT already carries the `enc` field (inbox.h:34), so flipping it is a parameter, not a store
  version bump.
- **DATA_TYPE 19 is free** (15 INTRO · 16 MOBILE_KEY_FORWARD · 17 SEALED_RELAY · 18 CHANNEL_POST).
- **The crypto core** (`dm_crypto`): X25519 → BLAKE2b KDF → XChaCha20-Poly1305, sealed-sender (the sealed
  payload carries the sender's authenticated identity), and the SEALED_RELAY precedent for a CARRIED
  nonce-counter when the frame's own ctr can't serve (the S4 ctr_M/ctr_H lesson — frame_codec.h:476).
- **Location machinery exists**: `DATA_FLAG_LOCATION` + lat_e7/lon_e7 + loc_in_dm/loc_in_m config.
- **Team provisioning**: `team new key=… freq= sf= bw= sf_list=…` (kv_next-tokenized since 3-A), persisted
  via the NV Blob (kVersion bump pattern, src/device_nv.h:22). Companion contract: team_reg push, team_id
  channel tagging already live.
- **Channel flood/relay path is content-blind** (the flood machinery keys on channel_msg_id + coverage,
  never the body) — MUST be verified by the coder at implementation (a relay without team_priv must
  forward encrypted posts unimpeded; nothing in handle_flood/digest/pull may need the plaintext).

## 2. Design

### 2.1 The team channel keypair

> ## ★ OWNER RULING 2026-07-29 — GENERATION IS UNCONDITIONAL; `genkey` IS REMOVED
> *"when team is created — a dedicated pair of key has to be created."*
> **`team new` ALWAYS mints the keypair.** The `genkey` opt-in below is **superseded**; ignore it wherever it
> appears in this document (§2.1, §3/T-K1).
> **Rationale:** an opt-in flag is a footgun — create a team, forget the flag, and you discover only later that
> you can neither encrypt nor grant. It also makes **§2.5 coherent**: `team_channel_crypt` defaults **ON**, which
> is incoherent if the creator might hold no key at all.
> **Unchanged by this ruling:** `team <id>` (**join**) still generates **NOTHING** — a joiner receives the key by
> grant (§2.3/T-K3) or QR (§2.4/T-K4). ★ That is not merely a design preference: **s34 is the only scenario that
> invokes the verb and it uses the join form**, so keygen-on-join would consume shared-RNG draws and could
> re-anchor the whole corpus. `team new tkpub=…/tkpriv=…` **adopts** an existing key instead of minting one
> (idempotent with QR onboarding).

- X25519 keypair (`team_ch_pub[32]`, `team_ch_priv[32]`), generated ON the creator's node at
  ~~`team new … genkey`~~ **`team new` (unconditionally — see the ruling above)** (device crypto RNG; the sim
  wrapper's seeded RNG path for determinism in tests).
- Stored in NV (two new 32-B fields + a has-key flag; **kVersion bump** — standard reprovision-on-reflash).
- Distinct from every existing key: NOT the node identity, NOT the team_id derivation input. team_id and
  overlay membership are unchanged — this key gates CONTENT only.

### 2.2 Encrypted channel posts (the wire)
- New flavor value/bit: `channel_flavor_crypted` — set TOGETHER with the team bit for v1 (encrypted
  NON-team channels are out of scope; reject the combination crypted+!team at origination, fail loud).
- Body = the sealed blob: seal the inner plaintext to **team_ch_pub** using the EXISTING sealed-sender
  path (the team is "a recipient" — reuse, don't fork: U2). Everyone holding team_ch_priv opens; the
  sealed-sender interior gives the authenticated member identity (receivers verify the sealed source
  against the team key cache — anti-spoof within the team).
- **Nonce/ctr hazard (the S4 lesson — design against dm_crypto's ACTUAL code):** `dm_nonce` binds a ctr;
  a channel post's natural identity is the 32-bit channel_msg_id (origin‖hash16‖ctr). The coder must
  derive the nonce from material that is UNIQUE per post and AVAILABLE to every reader — the
  channel_msg_id + origin hash is the candidate; if it doesn't fit dm_nonce's shape, CARRY a seal_ctr in
  the body exactly as SEALED_RELAY does. This is the spec's #1 review-at-implementation point.
- ⚠⚠ **CORRECTED 2026-07-31 BY OWNER RULING — THIS FIELD MUST BECOME A FLAGS BYTE, NOT AN ENUM.** The owner wants
  **`send_channel -t -l -e`**: *"sending together with channel encrypted message my location."* The design below is an
  **XOR** (`0` = text **or** `1` = location), so **text + location is NOT REPRESENTABLE** — the requirement cannot be met
  by adding a console flag. **See `2026-07-30-channel-crypt-and-location-privacy-design.md` §2.2.1** for the full
  reasoning and the three encodings weighed.
  ★★ **Take the FLAGS form:** `[flags u8][location 6 B if bit1][text if bit0]` — `bit0` = text present, `bit1` = location
  present. **Each FEATURE costs a bit; every COMBINATION is free.** An enum would need a codepoint per *combination*
  (text, loc, text+loc, telemetry, telemetry+loc, …) — **which is exactly how `q_opcode` (2 bits) and the DATA flags byte
  (`0xFF`) both reached exhaustion in this project.** Layout mirrors the DATA inner: **fixed-size field first, variable
  last.** ⚠ **`flags == 0` must be REFUSED** (an empty post is a bug).
  ★ **AND USE `pack_loc6` — 6 BYTES, NOT the 8 sketched below.** The DM path already carries a 6-byte location
  (~11 m quantisation); two encodings on two planes would force the companion to carry two decoders and make one plane
  silently more precise (U1).
  ⚠ **This is a WIRE decision and it must be settled WHEN CL2/T-K2 BUILDS IT** — changing it afterwards is a wire change,
  and the retired-codepoint rule then applies forever.
  *(original text, kept as the record:)* Inner plaintext = `[inner_type u8][payload]`: `0` = text, `1` = **location**
  (lat_e7 i32, lon_e7 i32, optional alt/flags — reuse the existing location encoding; the companion renders members on a
  map). Room for future inner types (telemetry, waypoints).
- **Un-keyed receiver**: relays normally (content-blind), but on DELIVERY-to-self with no team_ch_priv →
  drop with a loud emit + a rate-limited Push (`team_channel_no_key`) so the app can prompt "ask a
  teammate for the key". NOT stored-for-later in v1 (Open Question Q1).
- Inbox: `record_channel(..., enc=1)` — the format supports it today.

### 2.3 The key-grant DM — `DATA_TYPE_TEAM_KEY_GRANT = 19`
- Body = `[team_id u32][name_len u8][team_name ≤32][team_ch_pub 32][team_ch_priv 32]` (Q2 ruling: team_id
  carried defensively — the receiver REFUSES a grant whose team_id mismatches its current team, fail loud;
  team_name rides so the app can label the key).
- **MUST travel sealed**: origination REFUSES to send TYPE-19 in a plaintext DM (fail loud — C2). It rides
  the existing sealed machinery (same-layer sealed, SEALED_RELAY for XL/delegated) — no new transport.
  Prereq: the granter holds the newjoiner's pubkey (the existing reqpubkey/INTRO machinery; the companion
  flow: newjoiner appears in the team roster → member taps "grant key").
- Receiver: validate lengths, store to NV (kVersion-bumped fields), emit + Push `team_key_received{name}`;
  idempotent re-grant = overwrite (re-keying uses the same verb).
- Console: `team grantkey <0xhash>` (kv/flag conventions per 3-A). Companion JSON: a `team_grant` verb +
  the `team_key_received` push — contract additions land with the slice.
- Any keyholder may grant (trust model above). No creator-only enforcement — enforcement would be
  theater anyway (a keyholder can always exfiltrate priv).

### 2.4 The team QR (companion, out-of-band)
_Companion-side surface indexed in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-26-companion-v1-feature-roadmap.md` §A._
- One scan = full onboarding: overlay parameters AND the content key.
- Payload (versioned, compact — CBOR or a fixed packed struct, companion's choice): `ver u8 · team_name ·
  team_id u32 · freq_khz u32 · bw_hz u32 · routing_sf u8 · sf_list bitmap u16 · cr u8 · team_ch_pub 32 ·
  team_ch_priv 32` (~90 B + name → comfortable QR). Include a short integrity check (CRC32) — QR scans
  corrupt.
- App flow: creator's phone renders it behind a deliberate "Share team" screen (it contains a PRIVATE
  key — the UI must say so); joiner scans → the app provisions its node over the existing companion
  channel (`team …` + the new key fields, one new/extended JSON verb). Firmware side: extend `team` with
  optional `tkpub=`/`tkpriv=` hex64 params (idempotent with grant-by-DM).
- The QR path needs NO new radio protocol — it is provisioning.
- ★ **EXPORT — DEFINED BY OWNER RULING 2026-07-29 (the spec previously left this gap, and T-K1 flagged it as a
  T-K4 blocker).** The app obtains the pair from the creator's node via a new console verb **`team exportkey`**,
  answering `{"ev":"team_key_export","team_id":…,"tkpub":"<64 hex>","tkpriv":"<64 hex>"}`. **Available on EVERY
  transport** — the owner was offered disclose-once-at-mint and USB-only, with the exfiltration risk stated, and
  chose any-transport deliberately. ⚠ It must **NOT** ride `ready` (unsolicited, fires every connect); explicit
  verb only. ⚠ **ACCEPTED RISK + its consequence:** anything that reaches the console can read the content key
  silently, so the standing *"BLE fallback exposes the full console"* watch-item is **promoted to a dependency of
  this feature** — it is now the only control over the team key. Full wording in
  `ios-companion/INBOX_SYNC_CONTRACT.md` §"node → app: export the team channel keypair".

### 2.5 Send-side defaults
Mirror the DM pattern (`e2e_dm`): new config `team_channel_crypt` default ON — when the node HOLDS a team
key, `send_channel -t` seals by default; a plaintext team post requires an explicit opt-out flag. A node
WITHOUT the key sends plaintext team posts exactly as today (unchanged, and keyholders still read them —
plaintext flavor is always openable). Recommend the companion surface the lock state per team.

## 3. Slices (gateable, sequential)

- **T-K1**: keypair NV storage + ~~`team new genkey`~~ **unconditional keygen at `team new`** (owner ruling 2026-07-29, §2.1) + `tkpub=/tkpriv=` provisioning params + companion
  contract fields. (No wire change; s18 byte-identical; native tests for NV round-trip.)
- **T-K2**: the encrypted channel flavor — seal/open path, nonce design (THE review point), un-keyed-drop
  push, inbox enc=1, `team_channel_crypt` default. Gate: s18 EXACT (team-gated), s22/s28/s29 forensics
  (their team posts flip to sealed where scenarios hold keys — scenario updates are QA-owned), new
  scenario asserts: keyed member reads, un-keyed OVERLAY member relays-but-cannot-read (the containment
  assert this whole feature exists for), off-team node unchanged.
- **T-K3**: TYPE-19 key grant end-to-end (same-layer + XL/delegated sealed transport) + `team grantkey` +
  pushes. Gate: a scenario where a newjoiner team-DADs in, CANNOT read, receives the grant via sealed DM,
  then reads — the feature's acceptance story in one scenario.
- **T-K4** (companion repo): QR encode/decode + the share/scan flows + provisioning verb. Firmware-side
  acceptance = T-K1's params already cover it.
- **T-K5**: the location inner-type + companion map rendering.

## 4. Open questions — ★ ALL RULED by the owner 2026-07-26 (recorded verbatim next to each)

- **Q1 — RULED: drop-loud.** An un-keyed member receiving an encrypted post drops it with the loud emit +
  rate-limited Push; no store-for-later in v1.
- **Q2 — RULED: include team_id AND team_name in the grant body** (both now in §2.3); refuse on
  team_id mismatch.
- **Q3 — RULED: explicit opt-out flag.** With a key held, `send_channel -t` seals by default; a plaintext
  team post requires the explicit flag (mixed keyed/un-keyed teams stay usable during rollout).
- **Q4 — RULED: app-driven per-send** in v1 (a firmware periodic knob is a v2 candidate).
- **Q5 — RULED: yes** — re-key (generate new + re-grant) is the v1 revocation story, documented in the
  contract.

## 5. Out of scope (v1, explicit)

Forward secrecy / ratcheting; per-member revocation; encrypted NON-team channels (the flavor supports it
later); multi-team membership; key escrow. The wire cost of the sealed overhead (~50 B/post) is accepted —
team channels are low-rate.
