**refused LOUD** (`send_failed`, nothing aired) — **exactly as its non-colliding twin already was**,
because an unregistered mobile has no routable home and its E2E ack can never return. Both arms of the
loop now agree. This is a *consequence of selecting the correct routing plane* (the carve-out STOP
condition 1 allows) and is C2-correct; it is recorded here and in the corrected `node_mac.cpp` comment.
**It is unreachable in the corpus** (arm 4's four occurrences all have `team_peer == 0`), which is why
36/36 identity and this behaviour change are both true at once.

### 5f. End to end through the production receive path (case 0d/8)

The colliding mobile's wrapper is taken from its own flight (`parse_unicast_inner` on `pt->inner`),
checked to carry `DST_HASH = target` and `SOURCE_HASH = the mobile's stable hash`, and then handed to a
**real home node hosting that mobile** through `do_post_ack` (`drive_post_ack_mobile_send`). The home
**re-originates to the real target** (`pt->dst == 70`) carrying `DATA_FLAG_SOURCE_HASH`. The wrapper
reaches the home and is unwrapped; it does not reach teammate 77.

### 5g. Why the sealed arm needed a HAL seam (a measured finding, not a preference)

`TestHalBase::rand_bytes` returns all-zero by design, which `e2e_seal_inner` refuses (the R7 bad-RNG
guard) — the sealed arm produced `e2e_no_pubkey` and no frame. The file's usual spelling,
`hal._rand_ret = 7`, fixes the seed **but also forces every other `rand_range` draw in the send path**,
which was measured to push the flight's `next_attempt_ms` past `now` so `become_free` never issues it
(the wrapper sits in the queue and `pending()` stays null). `R0dHal` overrides **only** `rand_bytes`,
leaving every timing draw at its default. Both failure modes were observed and are recorded in the test
file's own comment so the next author does not rediscover them.
