### What the STOP was

`src/fw_main.cpp` refused **only the two BARE spellings** before the shared `dispatch()` fallback. Before 0a that
sufficed: `help messaging` matched no `dispatch()` arm (the help arm required `len == 4`), so BLE answered the
bounded `{"…","reason":"unknown_cmd"}`. After the split the router OWNS every `help <tail>`, so `dispatch` returned
true on BLE and the section streamed through `LineSink` → `mrble::tx_line` at ~20 B per notification — the same
self-inflicted flood the §B95 invariant-9 comment beside that guard exists to prevent, and the reason `peers all` is
refused two lines above it. **Measured on the defective composition: `help messaging` = 1365 B / 13 lines,
`help cfg` = 862–1673 B, `help zzz` = 149 B, all reaching BLE.**

⚠ Note the shape of the defect, because it is the reusable lesson: **neither half was wrong on its own.** The router
was correct, the guard was correct for the world it was written in, and their PRODUCT was the bug. No structural pin
on either half could have seen it — S10 and S11 were green throughout.
