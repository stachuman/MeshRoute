### 2.2 Recommended contract and regressions

Capture subprocess stdout/stderr as **bytes**, before interpretation. Retain both streams separately, with phase, exit status, byte length and SHA-256; retain complete evidence through the worker-to-parent transfer and before scratch cleanup. A bounded, ASCII-safe escaped view is for diagnostics only. Neither `errors="ignore"` nor replacement characters are an authoritative capture. Literal backslashes and nonprintable/high bytes must be unambiguous in the escaped view. Valid UTF-8 remains intact in the raw files.

Parse the ASCII doctest markers from raw stdout, or from one documented reversible view. Validate exit/summary consistency before crediting assertions: normal success/failure exits must agree with their summaries; signals, unexpected exits and absent/malformed summaries cannot be RED and must refuse a clean baseline. Build refusal stays distinct from run refusal. Do not relax native floors, match-count checks, worker completeness, restoration, or coverage to accommodate bytes. Keep ordinary ASCII diagnostics stable unless the brief explicitly records their changes.

The brief should cover these regressions through the **real functions/CLI paths**, not a second parser:

1. The 13 measured scenarios above, with stdout/stderr preserved byte for byte; include zero bytes, BB, C5, valid UTF-8 and a valid sequence split between writes.
2. Summary plus signal and unexpected-exit cases (B490); success/failure exit agreement. A control that removes exit validation must fail an assertion.
3. Build errors and no-verdict runs retain phase and exit, and never get credited as assertion failures. A build failure must not launch the old native executable.
4. Output exceeding 64 KiB and a cut multibyte boundary retain **all raw bytes**, while the human excerpt remains bounded.
5. Real worker serialization, parent retention and cleanup: both streams survive, hashes match, and no evidence exists only in a deleted worker. Exercise multiple workers and a non-ASCII diagnostic at the outer pipe.
6. Controls restoring strict decoding and removing a raw-evidence write must make the regression suite fail; an uncaught decoder exception is a failed regression, never a successful mutation verdict. Existing unusable-reason self-test witnesses remain effective.

`tools/test_mutation_unusable_reason.py` has **7 cases** and AST-extracts exactly `SuiteFailure` and `run_suite`, with a fixed namespace and string-returning subprocess doubles. Fence it for the byte result/helper dependency updates and real-child cases; keep its checks meaningful. Its source controls target the build classification and `retain_unusable` write. `tools/test_worker_formula_derived.py` has **7 cases** but is **not an AST reader**: it uses regex/text extraction, controls the formula/banner and counts its three rendered uses. Leave it unchanged if worker sizing is unchanged; full discovery reruns it. Do not import the executable harness to census it.
